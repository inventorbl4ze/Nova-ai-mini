What is NOVA?

NOVA AI MINI is an offline-first knowledge assistant. It uses a built-in collection of information and question matching to provide answers.

It can help users explore science, technology, education, gaming, football, general knowledge and more.

Important information

NOVA does not use a live AI API. Its answers come from its built-in knowledge library and matching system. It cannot independently verify breaking news or guarantee that every answer is complete or current.
